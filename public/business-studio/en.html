HOW WE WORK

From discovery to handover and improvement.

  1. Discovery & scope

    We review your materials and workflows, then agree on deliverables, timing and acceptance criteria.

  2. Materials, website & workflows

    We align messaging and design, and build the agreed features and workflows.

  3. Testing & handover

    We test with sample data, incorporate client feedback and provide usage guidance.

  4. Operations & improvement

    We review errors, workload and follow-up tasks within the agreed scope and make improvements.

Domain, advertising, third-party subscriptions and API costs are itemized separately. Scope, schedule and fees are confirmed in the quotation and contract.

Before you get started.

Can you use our existing website and materials?

After reviewing the current structure and usage rights, we propose improvements to existing materials or a separate page.

Does AI run every task automatically?

We start with recurring tasks. We agree on review and approval procedures for fact-checking, external messages, publication and changes to amounts.

Please prepare a company or product introduction, workflows to improve, existing materials and your preferred timeline. Do not send passwords or customer lists.
